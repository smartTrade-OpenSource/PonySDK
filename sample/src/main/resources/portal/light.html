In the page's document

With isolation="none" the ticket renders in this page's document, not in a shadow root: an addon that searches the document (document.getElementById, jQuery) finds it. The styles meet: this page's broad rules reach the ticket where its own are not more specific, and the application's page rules (:root, body) are rewritten to apply to the element only.
